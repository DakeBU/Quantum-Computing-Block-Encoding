`<x,z|c|allZero> = delta(z,allZeroBond) * M_C(word(reverse_N(x)))(0,0)`.

Internal full wires are bond-low/data-high in chronological emission order; public wires are data-low/bond-high with chronological bit t placed at N-1-t.
