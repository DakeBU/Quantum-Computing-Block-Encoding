Expanded objects and conventions
--------------------------------
All dimensions are arbitrary natural numbers; zero is permitted. All amplitudes
and matrices in this target are REAL, including signed values. Matrix indices
are explicit finite sets. For an l-by-r matrix B, row-vector action is
  (v B)_b = sum_(a in Fin l) v_a B_(a,b).
No conjugation is used in this real module.

A core A of shape (l,r) is an l-by-(2r) matrix whose columns are PAIRS
(t,b) in Fin 2 x Fin r. Its bit slice is A[t]_(a,b)=A_(a,(t,b)).
finProdFinEquiv flattens this pair to b+r*t, with inverse division/remainder.
This is bit-major block indexing, not an implicit little-endian word encoding.

Chain 0 r r is the empty chain nil r; at length zero the two boundary
dimensions must coincide. A nonempty chain cons A C has head A:(l,m) and tail
C:Chain n m r, giving Chain (n+1) l r. Internal dimensions are dependent
indices and may vary; there is no fixed padded physical register in this target.

Word 0=Unit (ONE empty word, not no words), and Word(n+1)=Fin 2 x Word n.
The head core emits the first component. The contraction T_C(x) is an l-by-r
matrix: T_nil(*)=I_r and T_cons(A,C)(t,y)=A[t] T_C(y).
Hence contractions multiply in head-to-tail row-vector order.
This target does not prove an identification of Word n with a flat integer or
a physical-wire PrimitiveBasis; imported adapters do not become target claims.

RightCanonical(nil)=True. RightCanonical(cons A C) means A A^T=I_l AND the
tail is right-canonical. Expanded, each core obeys
  sum_(t,b) A_(a,(t,b)) A_(c,(t,b)) = delta_(a,c).
This is row orthonormality; it does not mean the rectangular matrix is square
unitary or its columns are all orthonormal.

RankReduced is a relation between ACTUAL chains. The terminal dimension is
unchanged. For source bond dimensions d_0,...,d_n=r, the new dimensions satisfy
  q_n=r, q_i=min(d_i,2 q_(i+1)).
These are dimension-selected thin widths, NOT the algebraic ranks of cores or
the contracted tensor. A zero or dependent core can retain positive q_i.
maxBond is the maximum of ALL bond dimensions, including both boundaries.

Absorption preserves the bit label:
  absorb(A,S)_(a,(t,c))=sum_b A_(a,(t,b)) S_(b,c),
so absorb(A,S)[t]=A[t] S.
mass(v)=sum_a v_a^2. chainMass(C,v)=sum_x sum_(b in Fin r) (v T_C(x))_b^2.
The terminal bond is summed in FULL. For r>1 this is the mass of the
word-and-terminal-bond amplitude table, not the mass in label b=0 alone.

Every public declaration, independently decoded
----------------------------------------------
1. CoreFactorization(A), for every l,r and core A: a DATA structure with
   R:l x k, Q:k x (Fin 2 x Fin r), k=min(l,2r), and proof fields
   A=R Q and Q Q^T=I_k. These equations are RETURNED proof obligations of
   the record, not additional hypotheses on a call to factorCore.

2. factorCore(A): produces that record with no rank/nonzero/positivity premise.
   It flattens paired columns by b+r*t, applies ConstructiveThinLQ.factor to
   the actual flattened matrix, and reindexes Q back to the pair carrier.
   The provider chooses inner dimension min(m,n). For m<=n it eliminates the
   transpose by explicit bottom-up adjacent real rotations, obtaining
   reduced(A^T)=O A^T, R from its prefix transpose, and Q from the first m rows
   of O. For m>n it returns R=A, Q=I_n. It does not select arbitrary factors
   from an existence theorem. Zero pivots are assigned a zero angle. The
   signed angle uses sqrt(x^2+y^2) and signed arccos, not a floating atan2.
   Noncomputable exact-real expressions are not a runtime or finite-bit claim.

3. Result(C), for every n,l,r and Chain n l r: returned DATA consists of
   q:Nat, R:l x q, D:Chain n q r, and proof fields RightCanonical(D),
   RankReduced(C,D), and forall x:Word n, T_C(x)=R T_D(x).
   C is the only substantive input object; right canonicality, reduced
   dimensions and all-word action are outputs, not assumed of C.

4. canonicalize(C): supplies Result(C) by structural recursion. At nil r,
   q=r, R=I_r, D=nil r. At cons A C, first canonicalize the tail to
   (q_tail,S,D_tail), factor absorb(A,S), and return
   q=min(l,2q_tail), R=R_head, D=cons Q_head D_tail. The proof is the slice
   absorption identity, the factorization equation, and associativity; local
   Q orthogonality supplies new canonicality. No full-state-action oracle,
   full-rank condition, supplied factorization, or normalization is a premise.

5. canonicalize_rightCanonical(C): every produced core has orthonormal rows.
   Ambient n,l,r are arbitrary; only C is provided.

6. canonicalize_rankReduced(C): the output actual chain satisfies the exact
   backward min recurrence and unchanged terminal bond described above.

7. canonicalize_action(C,x): for EVERY word, equality of WHOLE l-by-r matrices
   T_C(x)=R T_D(x). It preserves every initial and terminal matrix entry,
   including signs, not just probabilities or a chosen terminal projection.

8. canonicalize_maxBond_le(C): maxBond(D)<=maxBond(C), including boundaries.
   It is a dimension bound derived from RankReduced, not an arithmetic cost,
   minimal-rank theorem, or estimate of qubit/gate resources.

9. boundary(C,v): for arbitrary v:Fin l->Real, returns u=v R on Fin q.
   It does NOT divide by a norm or automatically make an unnormalized input
   normalized. Its sign is not constrained.

10. boundary_action(C,v,x): equality of WHOLE row vectors on Fin r,
    v T_C(x)=u T_D(x). All terminal coordinates are retained.

11. boundary_mass(C,v): exact identity chainMass(C,v)=mass(u), with no
    normalization premise and any r. Action transports amplitudes; composing
    local row-isometries identifies the new full table mass with mass(u).

12. boundary_normalized(C,v,normalized): IF chainMass(C,v)=1, THEN mass(u)=1.
    The unit FULL word-and-terminal mass is the actual logical hypothesis.
    It does not merely assume mass(v)=1, and it is not a normalization
    procedure for arbitrary data. Conversely unit mass(u) implies unit input
    full mass by declaration 11; this converse is checked in the consumer.

13. stateBoundary(C), for C:Chain n 1 1: boundary(C,all-ones vector on Fin 1).
    Thus u_a=R_(0,a). No normalization hypothesis is required to define u.

14. stateBoundary_action(C,x): for every Word n,
    T_C(x)_(0,0)=sum_(a in Fin q) u_a T_D(x)_(a,0).
    The scalar terminal sector is the WHOLE terminal carrier here because r=1;
    no postselection is used. No target-state equality is a premise.

15. stateBoundary_normalized(C,normalized): IF
    sum_(x:Word n) T_C(x)_(0,0)^2=1, THEN sum_a u_a^2=1.
    This is declaration 12 specialized to scalar boundaries, not a theorem
    that every scalar-boundary chain is normalized.
