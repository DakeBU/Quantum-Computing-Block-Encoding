Status: **proved_locally**, independent review and full integration pending.

the separately invoked exact-type/axiom consumer passed in24.531 seconds,
selecting that exact fresh output. All seven roots report only propext,
Classical.choice and Quot.sound. These times are not CPU or strategy comparisons.

The seven claims are power value/counter, actual cutoff value, grid-step value,
threshold-accept value/counter<=4, and rightEntry partial counter<=11. The last
retains both acceptors, actual coefficient read where present, and two products,
over every branch. An arbitrary coefficient Run is not silently made free.

The exact three executable-definition modules and their proposition-formation
probe accompany the proofs. Their previous C150/C155/C156 freeze is preserved in
the C158 archives. Those earlier unproved status fields describe their original
stage, not a retroactively rewritten result. The original C157 author freeze
is also exact, including its pre-compile status and complete derivation.

same-data/chain refinement,400/816 partial bound, rational bitlength/GCD,
