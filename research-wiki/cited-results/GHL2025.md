## Cycle 10 O_D^BS Source-Domain Audit Note

The source audit used arXiv:2506.20478v2.  Lemma
`Banded-sparse-access-oracle and resource cost` states the padded-register map
$|0\rangle^{n-l}|s\rangle^l|i\rangle^n \mapsto |r_{si}\rangle^n|i\rangle^n$.
The Robin one-term construction then says zeros can be included in the set of
nonzero elements and uses diagonal sparsity $\kappa$; Eq. `ROBIN clarified`
sums over $s=0,\dots,\kappa-1$.  Therefore
`bandedSparseAccessPaperValidCleanSource` is only a classifier for nonzero
stencil branches.  It is not sufficient as the faithful source domain for
`O_D^BS` unless paired with a separate contract explaining the zero-amplitude
branches.

The next required QBE artifact is an unused-branch extension contract.  Until
that contract exists and is proved or explicitly kept as an obligation,
`GHL2025.Lemma1.ODBS` remains `contract-only`; injectivity, dagger cleanup, and
unitary extension remain obligations.
