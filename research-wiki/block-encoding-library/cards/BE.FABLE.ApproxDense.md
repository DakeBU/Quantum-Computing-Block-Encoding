# BE.FABLE.ApproxDense

Priority: P2

Source: FABLE approximate dense block-encoding synthesis.

## Detect When

The target is a dense or moderately structured matrix, exact construction is
not required, and the task accepts an epsilon budget.

## Construction

Generate rotation angles for a dense-matrix block-encoding circuit and apply
compression/sparsification when the allowed epsilon permits it.

## Lean Integration Role

Initial ABEIS integration should treat FABLE-like synthesis as a candidate generator
and Qiskit/numerical diagnostic source.  A candidate is not certified until ABEIS
proves the corresponding exact or approximate block-encoding theorem in Lean.

## Proof-DAG Leaves

- finite matrix generated by angles;
- numerical/symbolic angle-to-entry bridge;
- norm error bound against target;
- resource score for compressed circuit.

## Resource Notes

Good for approximate dense benchmarks and software exports.  Not the first
route for sparse, algebraic, or partial-permutation targets.
