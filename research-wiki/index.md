# QBE Research Wiki

Persistent project knowledge for papers, reusable block-encoding constructions,
claims, experiments, and gaps.

## Main Retrieval Layers

- `block-encoding-library/`: route selector and reusable construction cards for
  partial permutations, LCU, product/tensor arithmetic, sparse-access Gram
  constructions, density/purification, dilation, QSVT consumers, and
  approximate dense/structured circuits.
- `technical-lemmas/`: external or standard lemmas used by active proof tasks.
- `paper-contributions/`: source-paper contribution maps, separated from
  external technical lemmas.
- `cited-results/`: citation and formalization status for external results.
- `retrieval-index/`: compact JSON summaries for upper/middle agents.
