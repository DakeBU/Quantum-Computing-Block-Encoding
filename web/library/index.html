What “complete” means here

The generator scans explicit public def, abbrev, opaque, inductive, structure, class, theorem, and lemma declarations. Compiler-generated projections are reached through their parent structure and are not double-counted. A renamed or unresolved Lean declaration fails the documentation build.

ASPBE means Automatic State Preparation and Block Encoding for Quantum Computing. The documentation organization was inspired by Sho Sonoda’s Lean Ridgelet project; declaration panels and selectable themes are provided by Verso Blueprint.
