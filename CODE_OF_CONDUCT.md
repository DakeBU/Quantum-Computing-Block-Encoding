# Project participation

ASPBE is a technical collaboration. Discussion and review should stay focused
on mathematics, code, evidence, and reproducible behavior.

Participants are expected to:

- give specific, actionable, and respectful feedback;
- distinguish mistakes in an artifact from judgments about its author;
- disclose sources, assumptions, conflicts of interest, and known limitations;
- preserve contributor attribution and upstream licenses;
- avoid harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or publication of private
  information.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers
may edit, hide, or remove disruptive material and may restrict participation
when needed to protect the project and its contributors.
