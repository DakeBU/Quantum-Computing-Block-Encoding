## Clean compression is not full-output cleanup

The LCU architecture is `P_lift† SELECT AMPLITUDE P_lift`, with the rightmost
factor acting first. PREPARE and its chosen adjoint act on the selector;
AMPLITUDE targets the coefficient under selector/system controls; SELECT
permutes the system under selector control. Preserving a control's basis label
does not mean that its reduced quantum state is unchanged.

`be-004` and `be-112` now explicitly separate four requirements:

- Global unitarity follows from the local tensor, block-diagonal and
  permutation certificates, with adjoint and product closure.
- A full selector inverse `QP = I` uniquely requires `Q = P†`.
- A prescribed clean projected block need not determine the final gate.
  For unitary `D` fixing the clean bra, `Q = DP†` preserves that block.
- Deterministic cleanup requires the separate no-leakage condition
  `(I - JJ†)WJ = 0`. Hadamard preparation followed by controlled `I/X` and
  Hadamard unpreparation violates it, despite every local unitary certificate.

Both revised questions keep the chosen source architecture. Alternative gates
test necessity; they do not silently redefine it. The two exercises share the
clean-row and cleanup counterexamples, while their main stage-action and global
unitarity tasks differ. Their inherited train/dev assignment is **not** a
clearance for independent evaluation of those shared answer fragments. Global
semantic and split reconciliation remains open; do not pool their overlapping
subtargets as independent held-out evidence.

## Domains, quantifiers and finite permutation arguments

the old absolute-value wording. Its two real orthogonal completions are valid;
off-block entries remain coupled by orthogonality, not independently free.
Clean-output-only basis action contains the same information as compression,
not the whole output vector.

`be-008` keeps rational entrywise algebra distinct from real completion
existence. A real square root need not be rational. In every auxiliary
dimension, a clean compression of a unitary is a contraction: adding signal
qubits cannot encode a scalar of magnitude greater than one at normalizer one.
The convention is `A = alpha B`; raising the positive normalizer changes the
required block `B`, rather than defeating contraction.

`be-009` distinguishes existence of a suitable support map from correctness of
an arbitrarily fixed map. A two-nonzero column cannot refute an at-most-one
premise. `be-012` retains finite square self-maps, where injectivity already
implies surjectivity; source proof interfaces are not strict necessity claims.
The raw clean permutation block is a zero/one partial permutation in both axes.
A common positive normalizer can rescale its target without creating arbitrary
relative weights.

`be-108` proves same-axis reindexing by a unital star-algebra isomorphism.
Independent row/column permutations also preserve unitarity; what generally
fails is treating their fixed two-axis map as that same algebra isomorphism.
`la-013` likewise distinguishes a wire-induced bitstring permutation from an

## Exact phase and resource scopes

`be-104` retains the bare chronological list product `C`. The actual compiler
refinement is `exp(i phi) C = P(E)` for the phase-aware program. The concrete
seven-wire witness has stored phase `45 pi / 8`, so its bare product is
`exp(i 3 pi / 8) P(E)`, not a zero/one matrix. Equality up to global phase is
not exact matrix equality, especially when forming controlled operations.

`be-119` distinguishes thirteen visible backend wires from a fully expanded
physical footprint including internal oracle workspace. The ten-label
transcript and the declared-nine/effective-ten projection arithmetic do not
prove matrices, inverse semantics, cleanup or block correctness. Budgets with
different scope cannot simply be added or subtracted into a physical total.
