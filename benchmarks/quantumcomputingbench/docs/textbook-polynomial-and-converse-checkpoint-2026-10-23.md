## Mathematical changes

Two explicit question-version successors correct State Preparation targets:

- **SP108:** the endpoint term has degree $i+k+1$, not universally $k+1$.
  The question preserves that false description for assessment, then asks for
  its counterexample, exact elevation and the correctly weighted source
  Bernstein coefficients. Endpoint evaluations are not excluded.
- **SP111:** squared affine sampling has natural degree at most twice the
  original natural degree. Zero step can collapse it to a constant or zero.
  The question now assesses the old equality and derives the exact discrete
  mass formula using a coefficient upper bound, without excluding degeneracies
  or identifying expression size with finite-bit runtime.

These are expressly different question contracts, not answer-only patches or

Distinct reviews bind both old and new contracts and every candidate field.
Existing Lean companions or reviews do not transfer automatically to the new
questions; the old contract hash fails companion validation.

Three unchanged-question answer successors repair quantum explanations and
grading together:

- **plan004:** zero ensemble outputs are not divided by their norms. Isometry
  freedom is separated from physically different ensembles; explicit spectral
  rotations and a rectangular isometry provide genuine witnesses.
- **plan007:** separable states have nonnegative swap overlap, but reversing
  that implication is not a proof. An explicit product with overlap $x$, its
  invariant twirl, uniqueness within the Werner family and closed separability
  establish the actual converse. No low-dimensional PPT shortcut is assumed.
- **plan307:** the pinned source uses $e_T=r_Tc_T$, so columns act first on
  column vectors. The proof establishes $c_T=0$ on the entire tensor space for
  too many rows before composing with the row sum. The old row-bound theorem
  was true; ambiguous order wording did not refute it.
