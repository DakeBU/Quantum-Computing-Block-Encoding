## Symmetry is not a support or statistics certificate

Three Quantum Information targets now expose the relevant quantifiers:

- **plan414, answer correction:** compact normalized Haar yields the right
  translation needed for channel covariance. An invariant input gives an
  invariant output, whose symmetric compression is scalar by irreducibility.
  Covariance alone does not make arbitrary outputs or the whole channel scalar.
- **plan415, explicit question version 2:** permutation averaging preserves each
  product-state outcome law. Unitary averaging instead preserves a uniform
  worst-case spectrum-risk guarantee through an orbit average; it can change
  probabilities at a fixed state. Both commutations give weak Schur measurement
  plus classical postprocessing. The question supplies the performance criterion
  missing from the predecessor's unqualified WLOG claim.
- **plan420, explicit question version 2:** occupation orbits prove the symmetric
  dimension formula. A permutation-invariant mixed state need not be supported
  in the original symmetric subspace. The mixed de Finetti bridge uses a
  normalized purification in the symmetric space of paired system/reference
  copies, of local dimension `d^2`. Polynomial dimension alone does not make all
  dimension ratios close to one: for fixed `d >= 2`, the ratio
  `q_d(m)/q_d(m+k)` tends to one precisely when `k=o(m)`. The projected cloning
  core has `tr K(X)=tr(Pi_N X)` on the full algebra; full-channel completion and
  symmetric output range are distinct properties.

Zero copies, one-dimensional carriers and zero-dimensional state boundaries are
explicit. The exponentially growing quantity is ambient Hilbert-space dimension,
not a finite count of all states. No efficient circuit implementation follows
merely from the representation-theoretic reduction.

## A selected block has a scale and an access contract

**plan509** is an answer correction under the unchanged balanced-combiner
question. The clean selected ports are `(E+O)/(2c)` and `(E-O)/(2c)`. They can
coincide on allowed degenerate spectra; the factor two is forced by this fixed
balanced selector and projector, not a universal minimality or exclusive-success
theorem. Input rescaling, compatible branch flags and zero branches are explicit.

**plan510** has an explicit question-version change because one input ancilla
does not imply unit block factor or zero error. `A=2I`, `U=I`, factor two and
`f(x)=x` refute the old unconditional factor-one output. Under the stated exact
unit-normalized case, phase-aware coefficient LCU and binary reuse give the
conditional resource theorem, including the left factor `U R` for odd parity.
An exact factor-`kappa` input directly transforms `A/kappa`; recovering the desired
transform of `A` requires reexpanding `f(kappa x)` with a generally changed
coefficient norm. Approximate-input robustness is a separate obligation.

These are projected blocks, not automatically normalized whole output states.
Query counts do not pay for coefficient states, arbitrary gates, precision,
input preparation, success amplification or readout.

**plan517** separately corrects the real/complex domain question. Real inverse
sine is undefined outside `[-1,1]`; this does not refute the valid all-complex
odd multiple-angle identity. The negative exterior hyperbolic formula includes
its parity sign. Analytic continuation does not create a physical normalized
overlap greater than one, and an exact phase is not silently discarded under
control.

The primary sources themselves are assessed, not treated as infallible. For
example, a conditional all-zero transcript does not imply the printed stronger

iterations. These corrections include derivations and counterexamples rather
than merely replacing the reported final number.
