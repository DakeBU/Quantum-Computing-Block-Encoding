## State the actual field and dimension theorem

**plan106:** arbitrary algebraic complex irreducible representations of abelian
groups need not be one-dimensional. Multiplication by `C(t)^times` on `C(t)`
is a genuine infinite-dimensional counterexample. The corrected question asks
for that refutation and the positive nonzero finite-dimensional theorem:
commuting action operators are scalar by the eigenvalue/kernel Schur argument,
then any nonzero vector spans an invariant line. No finite-group averaging,
unitarity or topology is needed. The answer derives the multiplicative
character, its converse and the precise real quarter-turn and zero-carrier
boundaries. The exterior algebraic example is not a counterexample to a
properly hypothesized continuous/unitary theorem.

**plan108:** the trace character takes values in its scalar field `K`, so the
universal identity is `chi(e)=n*1_K`. This may legitimately be abbreviated as
dimension after a scalar cast; the correction does not falsely refute that
reading. Recovering integer `n` is a separate conclusion. Characteristic zero
makes the natural scalar map injective; characteristic `p` generally reveals
only the residue. Even the trivial representations on `F_p` and `F_p^(p+1)`
have the same complete trace character but different dimensions. Conjugacy
invariance and factorization through conjugacy classes hold over every field,
including the zero-dimensional case, without semisimplicity or finite `G`.

## Do not impose unnecessary restrictions to repair a proof

**plan112:** the algebraic flip is a commuting involution over any field and
dimension. When characteristic differs from two, its half-sums are complementary
idempotents with the specified eigenspace images. Metric orthogonality is a

**plan115:** its universal tensor-square reducibility target is true, so an

## Count the original entry interface

**plan209:** digital entry access supplies a coherent fixed-point record, not
free measurement or arbitrary-state copying. A phase-correct amplitude loader
can retain that record across the controlled signal operation:

```text
O ; C ; R_signal ; C^dagger ; O^dagger
```

Here `O` is the original entry query, `C` is explicitly costed reversible
arithmetic preserving that record, and `R_signal` reads the stored angle/phase
without modifying its controls. Both arithmetic branches and every scratch
register return clean. The ledger is one forward and one inverse original
entry query, `2G_C+G_R` other gates, all peak value/angle/phase/arithmetic
workspace, and a retained signal plus indices. Inverse access must genuinely
exist in the chosen oracle model; abstract unitarity alone does not supply a
free inverse query.

A separately clean angle oracle may internally use two original queries, so
naively bracketing a rotation by that oracle and its inverse may cost four.
Fusion can cancel the middle entry compute/uncompute pair by keeping the value
live. Thus two queries describe this construction, not a universal lower bound
or a ban on amortization. Relative phases, zero entries, safe stored ranges,
finite-bit versus ideal exactness and elementary synthesis costs are explicit.
A diagonal compression on the retained pair-index is not already a full
matrix block encoding; sparse access and outer compiler costs remain separate.
